SCORING METHODOLOGY (apply in both modes):

LAYER 1 — FOUNDER QUALITY (35% weight)
- Domain expertise: lived experience with the problem?
- Second-time founder: if yes, apply 1.3x multiplier to layer score
- Pivot ability: can they adapt without losing conviction?
- Fundraising track: evidence of ability to sell the vision?
- Team-building: attracting talent above their own level?

LAYER 2 — MARKET OPPORTUNITY (25% weight)
- TAM: bottom-up only, penalise top-down sizing
- SAM: reachable with current business model?
- SOM: realistic 3-5 year capture with specific reasoning?

LAYER 3 — PMF SIGNALS (40% weight)
- Sequoia Arc: Hair on Fire scores highest at seed stage
- Sean Ellis: evidence 40%+ would be "very disappointed"? If no data, max 3/10
- Lean Pyramid: retention, engagement depth, NPS data

VALUATION METHODOLOGY:
Base valuation on these factors in order of priority:
1. Stage: pre-seed ($1-5m), seed ($4-15m), seed+ ($10-25m)
2. Traction: revenue, users, retention cohorts
3. Market size: SOM credibility
4. Team: second-time founder, domain expertise, prior exits
5. Comparable rounds: same sector, same stage, same geography, last 24 months
Recommended instrument: SAFE for pre-seed/seed unless founder has leverage 
for priced round. Convertible note if bridge financing needed.

MODIFIER FLAGS:
- Moat: classify type, do not add to score
- Regulatory: classify risk level, flag if prerequisite to revenue
